# Real Films, original artwork

The Cinema Room recommends real, existing films, but every pixel depicting them is drawn for
this page: the Posters are original artwork in the film-poster idiom, and the projector plays an
invented Bumper and title card rather than any film's actual opening. Real posters, studio idents
and opening footage are owned by their studios, and this page is published publicly.

This is not a new compromise. The project already made the identical call for games — the
README describes the Scenes as "original fan art, not gameplay footage or official game
artwork", paired with real games and real Steam links. The Cinema Room follows the same
pattern: real recommendations, our pixels.

## Consequences

- Factual details about a Film — title, year, premise, why to watch — are accurate and are not
  artwork, so they are used directly.
- The Bumper is an invented studio with its own name, logo animation and music. It must not
  resemble a real studio's ident.
- Where a visitor wants the real thing, the page links out to official sources rather than
  reproducing them.
