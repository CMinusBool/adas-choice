# Three co-op games from C−'s Steam library

The ranking prioritises actual cooperation and the clever systems, tactics, and humour suggested by [C−'s public Steam reviews](https://steamcommunity.com/id/cminus/recommended/). All three games are in the supplied Games.txt. The original research was checked on 8 September 2026.

## 1. Operation: Tango — best overall

One player is an agent; the other is a hacker. You see different information, so explaining clues and synchronising actions are central to the puzzles. This is a strong match for the interest in programming and logic games shown in the reviews. Both people contribute to the shared “we cracked it” moment.

**Setup:** Online co-op only, with two devices and microphones. Your partner can use the free Friend Pass, so only one full copy of the game is needed.

![The agent and hacker solve a puzzle together](assets/operation-tango.gif)

[Open Operation: Tango on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1335790/Operation_Tango/)

## 2. Lovers in a Dangerous Spacetime — best tactical teamwork

Share one spaceship, dividing piloting, shields, and weapons, then switch stations as threats change. Planning and defence turn into a frantic, colourful joint effort. This is a promising fit for the enthusiasm for Company of Heroes and Dome Keeper shown in the reviews.

The profile records early two-player progress—5 of 28 achievements when checked—so this is a recommendation to return to a game already sampled.

**Setup:** Local co-op or Steam Remote Play Together. There is no native online multiplayer. A strong choice when sharing one PC.

![The pilot and shield operator save their spaceship together](assets/lovers-in-a-dangerous-spacetime.gif)

[Open Lovers in a Dangerous Spacetime on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/252110/Lovers_in_a_Dangerous_Spacetime/)

## 3. Heavenly Bodies — best shared slapstick

Handle equipment and solve physical tasks in zero gravity. Anchoring a teammate, passing tools, and moving awkward objects together can make even small successes memorable. Choose this if both players enjoy laughing at mistakes: the unusual movement is deliberately challenging.

**Setup:** Local co-op or Steam Remote Play Together. Controllers are strongly recommended.

![An astronaut anchors her teammate while he retrieves a wrench](assets/heavenly-bodies.gif)

[Open Heavenly Bodies on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1138850/Heavenly_Bodies/)
